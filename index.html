About Us

We are a student-led magazine aiming to give students a platform to showcase creative work. Creativity is about more than just articles, and we are open to everything from short stories to poems to artworks. We want students to showcase their unique experiences and perspectives through their work and their creativity. By giving students a platform to share their work, we aim to be able to help others view unique takes on each issue's theme. Carpe Noctem means "Seize the Night", and we believe that everyone has a story to tell, and we want to help you share yours.
